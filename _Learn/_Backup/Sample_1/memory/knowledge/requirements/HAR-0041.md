# HAR-0041 | Voltage Measurement and Monitoring Requirements

## Requirement

DP-XMC-5049-HRS-004: All voltages shall be measured by the multi-meter. Un-sequenced voltages shall be monitored through the voltage monitor section.

## Explanation

This requirement defines the verification and monitoring strategy for the power supply rails of the XMC module. It distinguishes between two types of voltage handling:

1.  **General Verification:** All power supply voltages must be verifiable using a standard multi-meter. This ensures that the hardware provides accessible test points or measurable nodes for all critical power rails during manufacturing or maintenance.
2.  **Continuous Monitoring for Un-sequenced Rails:** Voltages that do not follow a strict, defined startup sequence (un-sequenced) are considered higher risk for system stability if they fail or drift. These specific voltages must be continuously monitored by a dedicated hardware voltage monitor circuit (e.g., a supervisor IC or ADC-based monitoring system) rather than relying solely on manual measurement.

**Purpose:** To ensure all power levels are correct and to track voltages that are not part of the standard power-up sequence.

**Components:**
- Multi-meter (for manual verification)
- Voltage monitor section (for continuous automated monitoring)
