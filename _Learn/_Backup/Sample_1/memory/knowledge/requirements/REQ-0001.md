# REQ-0001 | Kintex Ultrascale+ FPGA Interface Support

## Requirement

Kintex Ultrascale+ FPGA shall support STANAG3350 / RGB / VGA, DVI, ARINC818 interfaces

## Explanation

This requirement mandates that the selected Field Programmable Gate Array (FPGA), specifically from the Xilinx Kintex Ultrascale+ family, must possess the hardware and logical capability to interface with and process multiple distinct video and data transmission standards. The FPGA acts as the central processing unit for these diverse input/output streams.

The supported interfaces include:
*   **STANAG3350**: A NATO standard for digital video transmission over fiber optic or copper links, commonly used in military avionics.
*   **RGB / VGA**: Standard analog or digital video signals used for display outputs.
*   **DVI (Digital Visual Interface)**: A video interface standard for transmitting uncompressed video data.
*   **ARINC818**: A standard for transmitting digital video and data over fiber optic links, widely used in commercial and military aircraft avionics systems.

The FPGA must be configured or designed to handle the protocol decoding, encoding, and signal conditioning required for these specific standards simultaneously or as selectable modes.
