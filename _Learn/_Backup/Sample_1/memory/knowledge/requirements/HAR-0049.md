# HAR-0049 | XMC Module Weight Limit

## Requirement

DP-XMC-5049-HRS-0022: Weight: <800g

## Explanation

The total weight of the XMC module must be strictly less than 800 grams. This constraint is critical for ensuring the module meets mechanical handling requirements, transport safety standards, and mounting constraints specific to the XMC form factor. Keeping the module lightweight facilitates easier installation and reduces stress on the carrier board connectors.
