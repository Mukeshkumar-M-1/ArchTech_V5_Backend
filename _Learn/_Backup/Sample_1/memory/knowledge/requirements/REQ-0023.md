# REQ-0023 | FPGA DVI/TMDS Video Input Limit

## Requirement

The FPGA shall be interfaced with a maximum of 3 DVI/TMDS video inputs.

## Explanation

**Meaning :** The FPGA connection is limited to accepting no more than three DVI or TMDS video signals as inputs.

**Parameters :**
- Maximum of 3 DVI/TMDS video inputs

**Purpose :** Defines the input capacity for video signals processed by the FPGA.
