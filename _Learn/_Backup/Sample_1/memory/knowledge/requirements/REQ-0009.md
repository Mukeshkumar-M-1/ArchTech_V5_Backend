# REQ-0009 | ARINC 818 Video I/O on XMC Secondary Connector

## Requirement

DP-XMC-5049-HRS-0011 ARINC 818: Three ARINC 818 Transmitter (Tx) for high speed digital video output on XMC Secondary Connector. One output channel shall be interfaced to XMC connector. Two output channels are optional with DVI channel 1. 4.25 Gb/s data rate (max) shall be supported for each Tx channel. Three ARINC 818 Receiver (Rx) for high speed digital video input. All three channels are optional with DVI channel 1. 4.25 Gb/s data rate (max) shall be supported for each Rx channel.

## Explanation

This requirement defines the high-speed digital video interface specifications for the XMC module, specifically utilizing the ARINC 818 standard. It mandates the presence of Transmitter (Tx) and Receiver (Rx) channels with specific data rate capabilities and connection constraints.

**Key Specifications:**
*   **Transmitters (Tx):**
    *   **Quantity:** 3 channels total.
    *   **Mandatory:** 1 channel must be interfaced directly to the primary XMC connector.
    *   **Optional:** 2 additional channels are available if DVI channel 1 is implemented.
    *   **Performance:** Maximum data rate of 4.25 Gb/s per channel.
*   **Receivers (Rx):**
    *   **Quantity:** 3 channels total.
    *   **Status:** All three channels are optional, contingent upon the implementation of DVI channel 1.
    *   **Performance:** Maximum data rate of 4.25 Gb/s per channel.
*   **Connector:** The video I/O is routed through the XMC Secondary Connector.

**Purpose:** To enable high-bandwidth digital video input and output capabilities suitable for avionics or high-performance computing applications, leveraging the ARINC 818 standard for reliable data transmission.
