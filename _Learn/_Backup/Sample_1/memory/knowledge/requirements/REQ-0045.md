# REQ-0045 | DVI Video Output Configuration

## Requirement

DP-XMC-5049-HRS-0010 DVI Video Output: Two single link DVI video outputs on XMC Secondary Connector. One channel is optional with two ARINC818 outputs and three ARINC818 inputs. One channel is optional with RED and BLUE signals of RGBHV channel 1 and 2. One single link DVI Video output on DVI Connector at Front Panel (optional).

## Explanation

This requirement defines the video output capabilities of the DP-XMC-5049 module, specifically focusing on DVI interfaces. The system is designed to provide flexible video output options through both the XMC connector and a front-panel interface.

The core functionality includes **two single-link DVI video outputs** located on the XMC Secondary Connector. However, the availability of these channels is conditional:
1.  **Conditional Channel 1:** One of the DVI channels is optional and requires the presence of specific ARINC 818 signals (two outputs and three inputs) to be active.
2.  **Conditional Channel 2:** The other DVI channel is optional and depends on the availability of RED and BLUE signals from RGBHV channels 1 and 2.

Additionally, the system offers an **optional single-link DVI video output** directly on the Front Panel DVI Connector, providing local access to video signals independent of the XMC backplane configuration.
