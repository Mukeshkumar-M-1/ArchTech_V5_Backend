# HAR-0038 | Operating Temperature Range at Card Edge

## Requirement

DP-XMC-5049-HRS-0015: Operating Temperature: -40°C to +71°C (Card Edge)

## Explanation

This requirement specifies the operational thermal limits for the DP-XMC-5049 module, specifically measured at the **card edge** connector interface. The module must maintain correct functionality and signal integrity when the temperature at this critical connection point ranges from **-40°C** (minimum) to **+71°C** (maximum).

This specification is crucial for ensuring reliability in harsh environmental conditions where the card interfaces with the backplane. The "Card Edge" designation implies that the thermal performance is validated at the physical interface where the module connects to the host system, rather than just the internal core temperature of the FPGA or other components. This ensures that the electrical contacts and the immediate surrounding PCB traces remain within safe operating parameters under extreme thermal stress.
