# HAR-0032 | Supply Current Measurement via Component Power Analysis

## Requirement

Requirement ID: DP-XMC-5049-HRS-005; Requirement: The supply current shall be measured by power analysis of module components; Testing Plan: NA

## Explanation

This requirement mandates a specific methodology for determining the total supply current of the XMC module. Instead of direct measurement using a current probe or ammeter on the main power rail, the current must be derived indirectly.

**Methodology:**
1.  **Component-Level Analysis:** Identify all significant power-consuming components within the module (e.g., FPGA, memory, transceivers, regulators).
2.  **Power Calculation:** Determine the power consumption ($P$) of each component under the relevant operating conditions (idle, active, peak load). This data is typically sourced from component datasheets or simulation models.
3.  **Current Derivation:** Calculate the current draw for each component using the formula $I = P / V$, where $V$ is the supply voltage for that specific component.
4.  **Aggregation:** Sum the individual currents to determine the total supply current.

**Purpose:**
This approach is often used when direct measurement is impractical due to form factor constraints, or to validate power budget estimates during the design phase before hardware prototyping. It ensures that the power supply design can handle the aggregate load of all internal components.

**Key Considerations:**
-   **Voltage Variations:** Ensure accurate voltage values are used for each domain, as voltages may differ across the module.
-   **Dynamic vs. Static:** Consider both static (leakage/standby) and dynamic (switching) power components for accurate current estimation.
-   **Regulator Efficiency:** If measuring input current to the module, account for the efficiency of onboard power regulators when converting component-side power to input-side current.
