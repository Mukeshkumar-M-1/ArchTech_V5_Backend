# HAR-0028 | XMC Secondary Connector Signal Definitions

## Requirement

DP-XMC-5049-HRS-0017: XMC Secondary Connector requirements: DVI channel 1 / 2x ARINC818 TX & 3x ARINC818 RX; 1x ARINC818 TX; DVI channel 2 / RGBHV channel 1 RED - BLUE & RGBHV channel 2 RED – BLUE; RGBHV CVBS Green channel 1 & 2; RGBHV channel 3 (Red, Green & Blue); One DisplayPort input channel; One optional USB channel from Micro-controller.

## Explanation

This requirement defines the specific signal mapping and pinout assignments for the **XMC Secondary Connector**. The connector serves as a multi-purpose interface handling high-speed video, avionics data transmission, and control connectivity.

The signal distribution is categorized as follows:

1.  **DVI Channel 1**:
    *   Carries video data for DVI Channel 1.
    *   Multiplexed with avionics data: **2x ARINC818 Transmitters (TX)** and **3x ARINC818 Receivers (RX)**.

2.  **DVI Channel 2**:
    *   Carries video data for DVI Channel 2.
    *   Multiplexed with avionics data: **1x ARINC818 Transmitter (TX)**.

3.  **RGBHV Video Signals**:
    *   **Channel 1**: Red and Blue components.
    *   **Channel 2**: Red and Blue components.
    *   **CVBS Green**: Separate Green channels for Channel 1 and Channel 2 (likely for composite sync or specific color correction).
    *   **Channel 3**: Full RGB components (Red, Green, and Blue).

4.  **Additional Interfaces**:
    *   **DisplayPort**: One dedicated input channel.
    *   **USB**: One optional channel connected to the Micro-controller, likely for configuration or debugging.

**Purpose**: To provide a consolidated interface for extensive video output options, avionics data transmission (ARINC818), and optional micro-controller connectivity through the secondary connector.
