# HAR-0033 | DP-XMC-5049 Power Dissipation

## Requirement

DP-XMC-5049-HRS-006 Power Dissipation • 15 W (TYP)

## Explanation

This requirement specifies the typical power dissipation characteristics of the DP-XMC-5049 module. The value of 15 Watts represents the heat energy generated by the module during normal operation. This parameter is critical for:

1.  **Thermal Management**: Determining the necessary airflow, heatsink size, or chassis cooling capacity.
2.  **Power Supply Design**: Ensuring the system power supply can provide sufficient current at the required voltage levels to support the module's consumption.
3.  **Reliability**: Operating within thermal limits to ensure component longevity and system stability.

The "(TYP)" designation indicates that this is a typical value under standard operating conditions, not a maximum limit. Designers should consult the datasheet for maximum power dissipation values if designing for worst-case scenarios.
