# HAR-0034 | Power Dissipation Measurement Method

## Requirement

DP-XMC-5049-HRS-006 Power dissipation shall be measured by power analysis of module components

## Explanation

This requirement mandates a specific methodology for determining the thermal performance of the module. Instead of relying solely on direct thermal measurements (such as temperature sensors or calorimetry), the total power dissipation (heat generated) must be derived through a bottom-up electrical analysis.

**Key Aspects:**
- **Methodology:** Power analysis of individual components.
- **Scope:** All module components contributing to the total power budget.
- **Objective:** To accurately calculate the total heat load by summing the power consumption of each active and passive component within the module. This ensures that thermal management solutions are based on precise electrical data rather than estimates.
