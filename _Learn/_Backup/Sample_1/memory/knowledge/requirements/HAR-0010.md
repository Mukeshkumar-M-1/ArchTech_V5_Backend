# HAR-0010 | XMC Module Clock and Power Generation

## Requirement

Module shall support on board clock generation and power supply generation as per design requirements. Module shall be powered by 5V, 3.3V and 3.3V-AUX via XMC Connector as per VITA 42 standard.

## Explanation

This requirement defines the internal power and timing architecture for the XMC module. It mandates two key capabilities:
1.  **Internal Generation:** The module must generate its own necessary clock signals and regulate power supplies internally, rather than relying on the backplane for these specific functions.
2.  **External Power Input:** The module receives its primary energy from the XMC connector, strictly adhering to the **VITA 42** standard. The specific input voltages required are 5V, 3.3V, and 3.3V-AUX.

This ensures that the module is self-sufficient in terms of timing and power distribution while maintaining compatibility with standard XMC chassis power delivery systems.
