# HAR-0002 | XMC PCIe Video Converter Module with Kintex Ultrascale FPGA

## Requirement

DP-XMC-5049 shall be a XMC PCIe based video converter module using XILINX Kintex Ultrascale FPGA Supporting STANAG3350 / RGB / VGA, DVI, ARINC818 and PCIe Interfaces. It shall be a Single-Width Mezzanine Card with the primary XMC connector occupying connector location P15 and secondary XMC connector occupying connector location P16. Module Kintex Ultrascale FPGA shall be interfaced with two 32-bit 4GB DDR4 SDRAM controllers, 64MB SPI NOR configuration Flash and 256MB SPI NOR storage flash.

## Explanation

This requirement defines the core hardware specifications for the DP-XMC-5049 module, a high-performance video processing unit designed for XMC (Xtended Mezzanine Card) form factors. The module serves as a bridge between various video input standards and PCIe-based host systems.

**Key Technical Aspects:**
1.  **Core Processing:** The central processing unit is an **XILINX Kintex Ultrascale FPGA**, chosen for its high throughput and flexibility in handling complex video signal conversions.
2.  **Form Factor & Connectivity:** It adheres to the **Single-Width Mezzanine Card** standard. Physical integration is strictly defined by two XMC connectors:
    *   **P15:** Primary XMC connector (main interface).
    *   **P16:** Secondary XMC connector (auxiliary interface).
3.  **Video Standards Support:** The FPGA is programmed to support a wide range of video protocols, ensuring compatibility with diverse avionics and industrial video sources:
    *   STANAG3350 (Military video standard)
    *   RGB / VGA (Analog/Standard digital)
    *   DVI (Digital Visual Interface)
    *   ARINC818 (Avionics Full-Duplex Switched Ethernet for video)
    *   PCIe (Host interface)
4.  **Memory Architecture:** To support high-bandwidth video processing and system booting, the FPGA is directly interfaced with:
    *   **DRAM:** Two 32-bit controllers, each managing 4GB of DDR4 SDRAM (Total 8GB), providing necessary frame buffer and processing memory.
    *   **Configuration Storage:** 64MB SPI NOR Flash for storing the FPGA bitstream and configuration data.
    *   **System Storage:** 256MB SPI NOR Flash for general data storage or firmware updates.
