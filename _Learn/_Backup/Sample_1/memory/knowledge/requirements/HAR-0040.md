# HAR-0040 | Relative Humidity Specification

## Requirement

DP-XMC-5049-HRS-0024: Relative Humidity: 95% RH

## Explanation

The hardware module is required to withstand high levels of ambient moisture without sustaining physical damage or functional degradation. This specification defines the upper limit of relative humidity the device can endure, ensuring operational integrity and longevity in humid environments.

**Key Parameters:**
- **Metric:** Relative Humidity
- **Limit:** 95% RH

**Purpose:** To guarantee that the hardware remains functional and undamaged when exposed to high humidity conditions, preventing issues such as corrosion, condensation-related short circuits, or material swelling.
