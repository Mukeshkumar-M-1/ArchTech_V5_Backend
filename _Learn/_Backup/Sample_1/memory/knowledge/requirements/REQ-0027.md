# REQ-0027 | STANAG 3350 / VGA Video Output on XMC Secondary Connector

## Requirement

DP-XMC-5049-HRS-0013 STANAG 3350 / VGA: Three STANAG 3350 / VGA / RGBHV / RGB Video output on XMC Secondary Connector. RED and BLUE of two channels are optional with DVI channel 2.

## Explanation

This requirement specifies the video output capabilities provided via the XMC Secondary Connector. The system must support three distinct video output channels capable of handling STANAG 3350, VGA, RGBHV, and RGB signal standards.

A key constraint or configuration detail is associated with "DVI channel 2." Specifically, the RED and BLUE signal lines for two of the video channels are optional when linked to or dependent on DVI channel 2. This implies a configurable hardware path or signal routing where certain color components may be omitted or repurposed depending on the specific DVI channel configuration.

**Key Components:**
*   **Interface:** XMC Secondary Connector
*   **Signal Standards:** STANAG 3350, VGA, RGBHV, RGB
*   **Channel Count:** 3 Video Outputs
*   **Conditional Logic:** RED and BLUE channels are optional relative to DVI channel 2.
