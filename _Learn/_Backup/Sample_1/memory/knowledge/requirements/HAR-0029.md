# HAR-0029 | Voltage Module Supply Rails

## Requirement

DP-XMC-5049-HRS-004 Voltage Module supply rails: • +5V • +3.3V • +3.3V-AUX

## Explanation

The DP-XMC-5049-HRS-004 module requires three distinct power supply voltage rails to operate its internal circuitry correctly. These rails provide the necessary energy for different functional blocks within the module:

1.  **+5V**: Typically used for digital logic, I/O interfaces, or specific analog components.
2.  **+3.3V**: Standard voltage for modern digital logic families (e.g., LVCMOS33).
3.  **+3.3V-AUX**: An auxiliary 3.3V rail, likely dedicated to specific peripherals, sensors, or standby circuits that require isolation or separate power management.

Proper power sequencing and stability on these rails are critical for the reliable operation of the XMC module.
