# ARINC 708 Test Specification

| Test cases | Description |
|---|---|
| **ARINC 708 Test** | Performed between 2 ARINC 708 channels. Data to be transmitted shall be framed as per protocol (1600 bits — 100 words). Both Tx and Rx channels shall be configured and data shall be transmitted from Tx to Rx. If both transmitted and received data are the same, test is PASS, otherwise FAIL. Provision to transmit multiple frames shall be available. |

> **Note:** Descriptions may be modified as per the specific requirement criteria.
