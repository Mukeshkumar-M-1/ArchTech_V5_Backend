# RS422 / RS232 / RS485 Test Specification

| Test cases | Description |
|---|---|
| **Loop Back Test** | Performed between 2 ports of different systems or the same system (loop back). Both Tx and Rx COM ports shall be configured and a buffer of data (256 bytes) shall be transmitted from Tx to Rx. If both transmitted and received data are the same, test is PASS, otherwise FAIL. |
| **Echo Back Test** | Connects Tx and Rx ports of one system (system1) to Tx and Rx of another system (system2). Both systems shall be configured with all possible combinations of 8 bits at selectable baud rates, with provision for configuring number of stop bits and enabling/disabling parity. A buffer of data (256 bytes) shall be transmitted from Tx of system1 to Rx of system2. The received data at Rx of system2 is again transmitted through Tx of system2 to Rx of system1. If both transmitted and received data of system1 are the same, test is PASS, otherwise FAIL. **Note:** For RS422 and RS485, one Tx may be connected to the available number of receivers subject to the maximum number as per standard. |
| **Parity Error Simulation** | Connects Tx and Rx ports of one system (system1) to Tx and Rx of another system (system2). Both systems shall be configured as odd and even parity respectively. A buffer of data (256 bytes) shall be transmitted from Tx of system1 to Rx of system2. The received data at Rx of system2 is again transmitted through Tx of system2 to Rx of system1. If parity error occurs, test is PASS, otherwise FAIL. |
| **Format Error Simulation** | Connects Tx and Rx ports of one system (system1) to Tx and Rx of another system (system2). System1 shall be configured as 8 data bits and system2 as 6 data bits. A buffer of data (256 bytes) shall be transmitted from Tx of system1 to Rx of system2. The received data at Rx of system2 is again transmitted through Tx of system2 to Rx of system1. If format error occurs, test is PASS, otherwise FAIL. |

> **Note:** Descriptions may be modified as per the specific requirement criteria.
